# Brand: tone of voice and key phrases

**Sources:** Paystack's own copy in `context/docs/research/voice.md` (homepage, About, Corporates, careers, support site, all checked 2026-11-12). Tone traits are INFERRED from that copy.

## Tone of voice

| Trait | What it sounds like | Evidence (Paystack's words) |
|---|---|---|
| Plain and direct | Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon in headlines | "Building a business is hard. Getting paid shouldn't be." |
| Confident, not loud | States what it does; ambition framed around customers | "Paystack is a growth engine for a new generation of innovative, forward-looking organizations operating in Africa." |
| Proudly African and ambitious | Africa named in almost every self-description | "We believe that in our lifetimes, businesses in Africa will routinely go toe to toe with the best companies on the planet, and win." |
| Warm and human | Talks about delight and care, not just features | "Give your customers the gift of modern, frictionless, painless payments." |
| Builder-friendly | Speaks to developers as peers | "If you can imagine it, you can build it with Paystack." |
| Honest and kind (values) | Transparency, clarity, kindness | Values: "Choose Transparency", "Communicate Clearly", "Practice Kindness", "Insist on High Standards", "Pursue Growth and Learning", "Embrace the Mission" |

## Key phrases (verbatim)

**Mission and identity**
- "Our mission is to help businesses in Africa become profitable, envied, and loved." (About)
- "Paystack's mission is to power African ambition." (careers)
- "Powering a new generation of businesses in Africa"
- "Modern online and offline payments for Africa"
- "A growth engine for modern businesses in Africa"
- "Paystack is a Stripe company"
- "Proudly built for Africa"

**Customers and promise**
- "Paystack helps businesses in Africa get paid by anyone, anywhere in the world"
- "Integrate Paystack once and let your customers pay you however they want."
- "ambitious businesses"
- "We're not just a payment processor, we're a growth engine for your business"
- "Start accepting payments in just 30 minutes"

**Product lines**
- "Delight customers with a seamless payments experience"
- "Build custom payments experiences with well-documented APIs"
- "Protect yourself and your customers with advanced fraud detection"
- "Detailed reporting for accounting, reconciliation, and audits"
- "Get settled within 24 hours" / "We settle all merchants before 10 AM, every morning"
- "The internet has no borders, and neither should your business."
- "Fair, transparent pricing" / "we literally only make money when you do"

**CEO voice (Shola Akinlade)**
- "We're betting on African entrepreneurs."
- "There are significant opportunities to support businesses beyond payments."

## Style rules for our copy

- No em dashes (CLAUDE.md), even where Paystack's own copy uses them.
- Reuse Paystack's phrases above verbatim or not at all; don't paraphrase them into near-quotes (INFERRED).
- Name payment channels concretely (card, bank transfer, USSD, mobile money) rather than "multiple payment methods" (INFERRED from the homepage channel list).
- Claims to avoid are listed in `context/messaging/messaging.md`.
