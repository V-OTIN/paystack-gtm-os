## Where Paystack wins

| Area | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Online checkout | Third parties: "Paystack owns the online checkout" |
| Developer experience | "Developers love our thorough, well-documented APIs" [voice.md]; HelloPeter reviewers cite "developer friendly APIs" [customers.md] |
| Settlement speed | "before 10 AM, every morning" [voice.md]; about one business day vs Interswitch's two (third-party) |
| Switching ease | "After we left our previous payment gateway for Paystack..."; "No bogus paperwork, just a few lines of code" [customers.md] |
| Multi-market under one provider | Live in five markets [company.md]; most local rivals are single-country (Hubtel, Yoco, Ozow, Nomba) |
| Brand and backing | "Paystack is a Stripe company"; Stripe, Visa, YC quotes [voice.md] |

## Where Paystack loses

| Area | Evidence |
|---|---|
| In-store / POS | Third parties: "Moniepoint owns in-store payments"; Paystack Physical Terminal is Nigeria-only [company.md] |
| Cross-border breadth | Flutterwave: 17 countries, 34 US Money Transmitter Licences; "handles large cross-border volume transactions" (third-party) |
| Bank-transfer-heavy collections | Monnify often recommended for bank-transfer-heavy businesses |
| Scale claims | Nomba claims 600,000+ businesses; Paystack's own figure is 200,000 or 300K [company.md] |
| Support reputation | Trustpilot complaints on slow support and pending payouts; Capterra support 3.9/5 [customers.md] |
| Banking bundle | Nomba, Flutterwave and OPay lead with accounts and credit; Paystack MFB is new (Jan 2026) [company.md] |

## How to respond

| Objection (illustrative wording, INFERRED; not a customer quote) | Response (INFERRED) | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| "We use Moniepoint for POS" | Don't fight in-store. Lead with online and in-app collections and payouts; offer Terminal only where relevant | Third-party framing; Terminal pricing [company.md] |
| "Flutterwave covers more countries" | Compete on the five markets that matter to the account and on settlement and reconciliation, not country count | Five markets [company.md]; "before 10 AM" [voice.md] |
| "Monnify is cheaper for transfers" | Compare total cost incl. failed payments and reconciliation time; Dedicated Virtual Accounts "1% per transaction (capped at NGN 300)" | [company.md]; reconciliation quotes [customers.md] |
| "Your support is slow" | Acknowledge; for high-volume merchants point to the dedicated Relationship Manager "well within SLA" | [voice.md] |
| "Nomba / OPay give us a bank account and overdraft" | Paystack MFB now offers corporate accounts and plans working capital loans | [company.md, voice.md] |
| "We're happy with our gateway" | Ask for a side-by-side on success rate and settlement; cite switchers' words | Switching quotes [customers.md] |
