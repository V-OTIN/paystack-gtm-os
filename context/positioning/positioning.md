## Positioning statement

For ambitious businesses in Africa that need to get paid online and in person, Paystack is the payments platform that lets them integrate once and accept card, bank transfer, USSD, mobile money and wallet payments, pay out instantly, and reconcile every transaction from one dashboard. Unlike stitching together cash, manual transfers and older gateways, Paystack settles merchants before 10 AM every morning, scales with high-volume businesses like Carbon and PiggyVest, and is a Stripe company live in five African markets. [voice.md, customers.md, company.md]

**Do not use in positioning until confirmed:** business count (200,000 on the homepage vs 300K on the careers site); Nigeria market share ("over 50%" vs "nearly 20%"); "the only payment gateway that lets you manage chargebacks efficiently"; "the highest transaction success rates in the market" [company.md, voice.md].
