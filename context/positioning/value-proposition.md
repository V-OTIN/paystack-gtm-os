# Value proposition by persona

**Sources:** `context/docs/research/` only, cited as [file.md]. Persona roles follow `context/icp/personas.md`. INFERRED = our synthesis of the cited evidence. Most customer quotes are undated and likely years old [customers.md].

---

## Champion: technical lead (CTO, lead developer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Ship payments into the product once and stop maintaining them. INFERRED from: "Integrate Paystack once and let your customers pay you however they want." [voice.md] |
| **Pain** | Missed payment confirmations, slow integrations, checkout drop-off. "even when the customer's browser fails to send us a notification" (CTO, GUO Transport); "It took my developer months to integrate our former payment gateway" (Moda by Maryam); "people no longer had to leave our website to pay" (CTO, Printivo) [customers.md] |
| **Gain** | One API for collections, transfers, customer data and identity checks: "Collect one-time and recurring payments from your app or website", "Make instant transfers", "Retrieve all your transaction and customer data", "Verify the identity of customers" [voice.md] |
| **Proof** | Webhooks (GUO Transport); "the easiest we've ever had on any of our technology providers" (CEO, Aella Credit); "the gateway into two different projects within 30 minutes" (CEO, Edves) [customers.md]. Plugins for Shopify, WooCommerce; Xero partnership [company.md] |

## Economic buyer: finance lead (CEO at founder-led firms)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Know that every naira collected is in the bank, on time, with low losses. INFERRED from the corporates offer [voice.md] |
| **Pain** | Reconciliation, chargebacks, slow settlement. "reconciliation was a big issue and the process used to take weeks" (St. Gregory's College); "we had so many chargeback issues" (Nairabox); "wait for about a week to get paid" (AdNaira, small business) [customers.md] |
| **Gain** | "We settle all merchants before 10 AM, every morning"; "Split payments into different bank accounts"; "Receive daily and weekly automated email reports"; "Bulk volume discounts", "Zero integration fee", "Zero maintenance fee" [voice.md] |
| **Proof** | "a significant increase in our loan collections" (Chief Risk Officer, Renmoney); "reconcile the amounts customers pay us with what we actually have in the bank" (Senior Finance Officer, Farmcrowdy) [customers.md]. Nigeria local pricing "1.5% + NGN 100", capped at NGN 2,000 [company.md] |

## Influencer: digital, product, growth or operations lead

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Give customers more ways to pay and keep growth from breaking the payment stack. INFERRED from influencer quotes [customers.md] |
| **Pain** | Payment failures, cash-only operations, vendors that can't keep up. "payment failures have reduced" (Chief Digital Officer, AXA Mansard); "Before Paystack, Taxify only accepted cash." (Bolt) [customers.md] |
| **Gain** | Channels: Card, Bank Account, Bank Transfer, USSD, POS, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa QR, Mobile Money; payments routed "through the most optimal channels" (Paystack's claim) [voice.md] |
| **Proof** | "scaling perfectly to support our large transaction volumes" (Growth Lead, Carbon); "within a month it shot up to the number one most used option" (CEO, Nairabet); "speed of delivery, execution excellence" (Chief Transformation Officer, MTN) [customers.md] |
