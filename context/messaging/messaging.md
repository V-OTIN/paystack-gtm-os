# Messaging

**Sources:** `context/docs/research/` only, cited as [file.md]. INFERRED = our synthesis. Use with `context/brand-kit/brand.md`.

## Core message

**Get paid by anyone, anywhere, and see every naira land: one integration for money in and money out.**

INFERRED synthesis of Paystack's own lines: "Paystack helps businesses in Africa get paid by anyone, anywhere in the world"; "Building a business is hard. Getting paid shouldn't be."; "Integrate Paystack once and let your customers pay you however they want." [voice.md]

## Pillars

| # | Pillar | What it means | Proof points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Payments that go through** | More channels, fewer failures | Nine channels incl. card, bank transfer, USSD, mobile money [voice.md]. "payment failures have reduced" (AXA Mansard); Nairabet: "number one most used option" within a month [customers.md] |
| 2 | **Money in and out on one integration** | Collect, pay out, split and verify through one API | "Make instant transfers"; "Verify the identity of customers" [voice.md]. PiggyVest instant withdrawals; Bolt driver earnings; Selar revenue splits; BuyCoins BVN lookup [customers.md] |
| 3 | **Books that reconcile** | Next-morning settlement, exports, chargeback handling | "We settle all merchants before 10 AM, every morning"; "Detailed reporting for accounting, reconciliation, and audits" [voice.md]. Farmcrowdy reconciles against the bank; St. Gregory's "used to take weeks"; Nairabox on chargebacks [customers.md] |
| 4 | **Built to scale with you** | Holds up at volume, across markets, backed by Stripe | Carbon "scaling perfectly"; Branch "wouldn't break at scale" [customers.md]. Live in five markets; volume up "more than twelvefold" since 2020 [company.md]. "Paystack is a Stripe company" [voice.md] |

## Words to use

From Paystack's and customers' own language [voice.md, customers.md]:
- get paid, settle, settlement, reconcile, collections, payouts, transfers
- seamless, reliable, easy, fast, secure, trust
- integrate once, well-documented APIs, webhooks, plugins
- ambitious businesses, across Africa, growth engine
- every channel by name: card, bank transfer, USSD, mobile money, Apple Pay

## Words and claims to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Em dashes | House rule (CLAUDE.md) |
| "200,000 businesses" or "300K businesses" | Paystack states both [company.md] |
| "over 50%" or "nearly 20%" of Nigeria's online transactions | Paystack states both [company.md] |
| "the only payment gateway that lets you manage chargebacks" | Unverified superlative [voice.md] |
| "highest transaction success rates in the market" | Unverified superlative [voice.md] |
| Diagnosing pain ("your payments are broken") | Infer situation, not feelings (signal-matrix-framework guardrail) |
| Customer results from small businesses in enterprise copy (30% recovery, 15% prepaid growth) | Wrong segment [customers.md] |
| Competitor bashing | INFERRED: use peer comparison instead |
