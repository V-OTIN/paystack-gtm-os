# Paystack: competitors

Raw research. Facts only, each with a source and the date checked. Headlines are verbatim from each competitor's homepage. Inferences are marked INFERRED.

## How the market groups them (third-party views)

- "Paystack, Flutterwave, and Interswitch dominate the B2B payment gateway space, while OPay, PalmPay, and Moniepoint lead consumer-facing mobile". Source: search snippet, https://techeconomy.ng/nigerias-top-10-payment-gateway-providers/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- "Moniepoint owns in-store payments. Paystack owns the online checkout. Flutterwave handles large cross-border volume transactions." Source: search snippet, https://brands.ng/blog/interswitch-vs-paystack-vs-flutterwave-how-nigerias-top-payment-gateways-really-compare-in-2026/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- BusinessDay frames Flutterwave, Paystack and Moniepoint as moving from payment volume toward control of financial infrastructure. Source: https://businessday.ng/technology/article/a-decade-on-flutterwave-paystack-and-moniepoint-compete-for-africas-financial-plumbing/ (2026-03-03, checked 2026-11-12)
- Semafor names Flutterwave, Interswitch, Moniepoint, OPay and Kuda as competitors. Source: https://www.semafor.com/article/01/20/2026/paystack-eyes-growth-beyond-payments-with-restructuring (checked 2026-11-12)
- Flutterwave is "Paystack's closest Nigerian rival". Source: https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/09/01/paystack-quietly-acquired-nigerian-fintech-allawee-and-is-now-shutting-it-down-to-build-something-bigger/ (checked 2026-11-12)

## Pan-African and Nigeria

### Flutterwave
- Headline: "Endless possibilities for all". Sub: "Empowering businesses of all sizes with seamless payment solutions tailored for enterprises, startups, and emerging markets."
- Claims: "30+ Currencies"; "20M+ API calls"; "500k+ payments daily"; "15+ payment options"; banking licence in Nigeria, "our largest market". 17 countries listed including UK, US.
- Nav: Enterprise, SME, Personal (Send App), Flutterwave MFB.
- Source: https://flutterwave.com/ng (checked 2026-11-12)
- Valued at $3B; 34 US Money Transmitter Licences; BCEAO payment institution licence (Senegal); acquired Mono in January 2026 (all-stock, $30M). Source: https://businessday.ng/technology/article/a-decade-on-flutterwave-paystack-and-moniepoint-compete-for-africas-financial-plumbing/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### Moniepoint / Monnify
- Homepages returned 403 (https://moniepoint.com/ng , https://monnify.com/, checked 2026-11-12). Positioning in their own words: **Not found.**
- Profitable; "over 800 million transactions monthly"; raised $110M (late 2024) at a $1B valuation, later closed at $200M; MonieWorld (UK); approved for majority stake in Kenya's Sumac MFB. Source: https://businessday.ng/technology/article/a-decade-on-flutterwave-paystack-and-moniepoint-compete-for-africas-financial-plumbing/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- Monnify is often recommended for bank-transfer-heavy businesses. Source: search snippet, https://kolonell.com/en/blog/payment-aggregator-comparison-nigeria-2026 (checked 2026-11-12)
- About 38.5% of the POS market (unsourced, interview). Source: https://businessamlive.com/cbns-payment-rules-signal-tougher-stance-on-fintech-market-concentration/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### Interswitch
- Headline: "Building the Rails of Africa's Digital Future". Sub: "Sustaining technology ecosystems that help commerce evolve, businesses grow and individuals thrive". "Two Decades Of Growth & Innovation", founded 2002.
- Segments: Individual, Small businesses, Enterprise, Developers & Aggregators. Verticals include Government, Energy, Mobility.
- Source: https://www.interswitchgroup.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- Settles in about two business days vs about one for Paystack and Flutterwave (third-party claim). Source: search snippet, https://brands.ng/blog/interswitch-vs-paystack-vs-flutterwave-how-nigerias-top-payment-gateways-really-compare-in-2026/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### OPay
- Headline: "We are Beyond Banking". "Licensed by CBN and Insured by NDIC." Consumer homepage; business site at opaybusiness.opayweb.com not fetched.
- Source: https://www.opayweb.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- About 27% of the POS market (unsourced, interview). Source: https://businessamlive.com/cbns-payment-rules-signal-tougher-stance-on-fintech-market-concentration/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### Nomba
- Headline: "Banking that moves your business forward." Sub: "Accept payments, manage multi currency accounts, and access overdrafts. Everything your business needs to grow."

## South Africa

### Yoco
- Headline: "Yoco runs your business, not just your payments." Sub: "Sell anywhere, run staff and stock from one screen. The all-in-one system for independent South African businesses."
- Claims: "250 000+ businesses trust Yoco"; "over R250 million a year" returned through rate cuts.
- Source: https://www.yoco.com/za/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### Peach Payments
- Headline: "The payments platform powering Africa's growth". Sub: "Turn your payments into a growth engine with better performance, greater control and one platform across your business."
- Claims: "One integration. 15 markets."; "PCI DSS Level 1 Certified." Strong plugin list (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, WIX).
- Source: https://www.peachpayments.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### Ozow
- Headline: "Accept payments, issue refunds & make payouts, easily & fast." Sub: "Ozow links businesses of all sizes in South Africa with 47 million bank account holders to transact simply and securely."
- Products include PayShap Request, Capitec Pay, Crypto Payments. "+6 mil Registered Users"; "150+ Ozow Employees".
- Source: https://ozow.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)

## Ghana

### Hubtel
- Headline: "When nothing blocks payments, growth happens." Eyebrow: "FOR SCALE-READY BUSINESSES IN GHANA". Sub: "Hubtel rebuilds how money moves through your business, so you can clearly see it, control it, and grow it."
- Claims: "7,979+ Businesses"; "4M+ Annual Users"; "20+ years of connecting businesses to communities." Has a "Switch to Hubtel" nav item.
- Source: https://hubtel.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)

## Kenya and East Africa

### Pesapal
- Headline: "Start Accepting M-Pesa and Card Payments". Sub: "Give your customers the flexibility and convenience they need by accepting both M-Pesa and card payments on one POS machine." "Regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya." 7 countries. Verticals include petrol stations, Oracle Hospitality.
- Source: https://www.pesapal.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)

### IntaSend
- Headline: "One API to collect and pay out across Africa." Sub: "IntaSend connects your product to mobile money and banks in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and DRC." Claims: "Businesses on IntaSend: 5,000+".
- Source: https://intasend.com/ (checked 2026-11-12)
