## Recurring words and themes

INFERRED from the quotes above (count of sources, not a style guide):
- "Africa" / "African businesses" / "the continent": in almost every self-description.
- "entrepreneurs" and "ambitious businesses": the CEO frames the buyer as an entrepreneur.
- "modern", "easy, fast and secure", "within minutes": speed and simplicity.
- "beyond payments": the 2026 strategic line.
- Tone: plain, short, confident; no jargon in taglines.
