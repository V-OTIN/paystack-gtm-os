# Paystack: buying signals

Public evidence of what makes a company need Paystack now. Raw research, each item with a source and date checked. Most "why this is a signal" lines are INFERRED, because Paystack does not publish its own trigger list. These are inputs to /signal-matrix, not conclusions.

## Evidence from Paystack's own product and pricing

| Signal | Evidence | Source (checked 2026-11-12) |
|---|---|---|
| Business is registered in NG, GH, KE, ZA (or CI) | "Our services are available only to businesses registered in" Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa and Kenya | https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130562 |
| Launching a website or app that takes payments | Merchants can start accepting payments "within minutes"; "no-code, low-code and pro-code tools" | same |
| Starting recurring billing or subscriptions | Features listed: recurring payments, subscription billing | same |
| Opening physical locations (Nigeria) | Physical Terminals only for registered businesses in Nigeria; NGN 86,000 device | https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2131842 |
| Needs to pay out at volume (gig, marketplace, lending) | Transfers pricing by band in NG, GH, KE, ZA; bulk transfers | https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130370 |
| Selling internationally / accepting USD | International and USD pricing columns; Kenya USD 3.8% | https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130306 |
| School collecting fees (Nigeria) | Separate schools pricing: "0.7% capped at N1,500" | same |
| Needs working capital (Nigeria) | Paystack MFB plans working capital loans, merchant cash advances, overdrafts using gateway transaction history | https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/01/19/shola-akinlade-paystack-targets-nigerias-32-billion-financing-gap-for-small-businesses/ |

## Funding

- Q3 2026: Nigeria led African funding destinations with $624M, then Egypt $396M, Kenya $297M, South Africa $279M (single source). Fintech: 47 deals, $230.2M (Briter). Sources: search snippets of https://blog.startuplist.africa/articles/q3-2026-african-startup-funding-analysis and https://businesstoday.co.ke/african-ventures-raise-us-674million-in-q3/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- Monthly lists of funded African startups exist, e.g. https://african-startups.com/news/funding/meet-the-african-startups-that-secured-funding-in-october-2026/ (not fetched, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: a newly funded startup in Paystack's markets is likely to be building or scaling checkout and payouts. Evidence that funding triggers a switch: **Not found.**

## Expansion

- Paystack is operational in Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, with Egypt and Rwanda pending. Source: https://techpoint.africa/news/paystack-creates-holding-company/ (2026-01-20, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: a business expanding from Nigeria into Ghana, Kenya or South Africa (or the reverse) needs local acquiring in the new market. A single provider across those markets is a plausible reason to choose Paystack. No Paystack source says this directly.

## Pain signals from reviews

- Recurring complaints: slow support, pending payouts (Trustpilot). Praise: developer-friendly APIs, wide payment methods, fast settlement (HelloPeter). Sources: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/paystack.com?page=9 , https://www.hellopeter.com/paystack (snippets, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: public complaints about a competitor's payout delays or downtime could be a switch signal. Not researched yet.

## Technographic

- BuiltWith tracks 11,321 live websites using Paystack, and the same tool can list sites using competitors. Source: https://trends.builtwith.com/payment/Paystack (snippet, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: sites on Shopify or WooCommerce in Paystack markets using a competitor gateway are a findable list (Paystack has Shopify and WooCommerce partnerships per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paystack).
