# Paystack: market and regulation

Raw research on regulation and market changes that may create urgency for Paystack's buyers. Facts only, each with a source and the date checked. Inferences are marked INFERRED. Most sources are press or legal commentary, not the regulators' own documents. Verify against the regulator before using in copy.

## Nigeria

### CBN payments circular: market structure, data localisation, UBO (June 2026)
- Circular title (as quoted): "Introduction of Market Structure Requirements, Data Localisation, Ultimate Beneficial Ownership Disclosure, and Systemic Oversight Measures in the Nigeria Payments System." Released Monday 15 June 2026. CBN reference number: **Not found.**
- Data localisation: "All payment transaction data generated in Nigeria must be stored and managed on servers within Nigeria." Deadline 1 January 2027.
- Market share cap: "Institutions exceeding 25% market share threshold in card issuing or merchant acquiring must achieve the required caps." Deadline 31 December 2026. Over 25% on one side caps the other at 15%, on a rolling 12-month basis.
- Merchant acquiring is defined to cover "PoS terminals, payment gateways and merchant settlement systems".
- Monthly market-share returns required; templates "yet to be issued" as of 12 Aug 2026.
- Paystack is named among firms expected to be affected, with OPay, Moniepoint, PalmPay, Flutterwave and banks.
- Sources: https://businessday.ng/news/legal-business/article/the-central-bank-of-nigerias-new-payments-system-circular-what-banks-fintechs-and-investors-need-to-know/ (2026-08-12), https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1714941-cbn-unveils-tough-rules-limit-opay-moniepoint-battle-pos-market-intensifies/ (2026-06-17), https://businessamlive.com/cbns-payment-rules-signal-tougher-stance-on-fintech-market-concentration/ (2026-08-31). All checked 2026-11-12.
- Conflict: Condia describes the cap as one-way (consumer issuing over 25% caps acquiring at 15%). Source: https://thecondia.com/fatf-cbn-fintech-regulation-nigeria/ (2026-09-22, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: The 31 Dec 2026 cap could force OPay and Moniepoint (reported POS shares about 27% and 38.5%) to restructure acquiring, which may push some merchants to re-evaluate their acquirer. Not confirmed by any source.

### POS rules (2026)
- "From April 1, 2026, a separate CBN rule forced every POS agent to be exclusive to just one principal." Source: https://businessamlive.com/cbns-payment-rules-signal-tougher-stance-on-fintech-market-concentration/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- May 2026: "Payment Terminal Service Aggregator (PTSA) certification requirement for every PoS terminal." Device-binding circular "took effect on July 1." May 2026: BVN-linked phone number changes limited to once in a lifetime. Source: https://thecondia.com/fatf-cbn-fintech-regulation-nigeria/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- Nigeria exited the FATF grey list on 24 October 2025. Source: same.

### Licence upgrades and capital
- CBN upgraded fintech and MFB licences: "Institutions like Moniepoint MFB, Opay, Kuda Bank and others have already been upgraded." National MFB minimum capital raised to N5bn from N2bn. Source: https://allafrica.com/stories/202601270517.html (2026-01-27, checked 2026-11-12)
- Paystack and Flutterwave both now hold microfinance bank licences (Paystack via Ladder MFB acquisition, Jan 2026; Flutterwave MFB in its nav). Sources: https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/01/19/shola-akinlade-paystack-targets-nigerias-32-billion-financing-gap-for-small-businesses/ , https://flutterwave.com/ng (checked 2026-11-12)

### Tax: e-invoicing under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025
- From 1 January 2026 VAT invoices are governed by the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, administered by the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), which replaced FIRS. Source: search snippet, https://invoicequickly.com/blog/invoice-requirements-nigeria (checked 2026-11-12)
- Deadlines by annual turnover, per BusinessDay (2026-06-29): Large (N5bn+) must transmit invoices via the NRS Merchant Buyer Solution by 30 June 2026, fines from 1 July 2026. Medium (N1bn to N5bn) enter from July 2026, enforcement January to March 2027. Small (under N1bn) later phase, enforcement from 2028. Source: https://businessday.ng/business-economy/article/june-30-e-invoicing-deadline-puts-nigerias-tax-reforms-to-test/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- Penalty: VAT on an untransmitted invoice may be treated as a fine; interest at 2% above the CBN MPR; input VAT claimable only on validated invoices. Source: same.
- Conflicts: other sources give 31 July 2026 for large taxpayers with a possible three-month extension, and 1 July 2027 for emerging taxpayers. Sources: https://sharedserviceslink.com/news/nigeria-s-large-taxpayer-e-invoicing-deadline-arrives , https://tryduplo.com/nrs-e-invoicing-deadline-nigeria-businesses (snippets, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: e-invoicing is relevant to Paystack only if its invoicing product supports NRS IRN/QR compliance. Whether it does: **Not found.**

## Ghana

- Bank of Ghana Notice No. 43, "Management of Merchant Collection Funds by Settlement Banks and Payment Service Providers", listed 6 November 2026, under Section 101 of the Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987). Merchant collections must sit in a designated settlement account; PSPs may not use, pledge or set off merchant funds; fees leave only as agreed with the merchant. Breach "may attract regulatory, supervisory and enforcement action". No compliance date beyond acting immediately. Source: https://www.riotimesonline.com/ghana-bank-of-ghana-merchant-funds-payment-providers (2026-11-10; AI-produced news article, notice itself at bog.gov.gh not fetched; checked 2026-11-12)
- Bank of Ghana published Corporate Governance Guidelines for PSPs in September 2026. Source: search snippet, https://www.regcompass.com/september-2026-regulatory-roundup/ (checked 2026-11-12)
- VASP Act 2025 (Act 1154) passed 19 December 2025; tiered PSP capital from GHS 0 to GHS 20m. Source: https://finhive.africa/news/regulation-fintech-guide-june-2026/ (2026-06-10, checked 2026-11-12)
- INFERRED: Notice 43 may lead Ghanaian merchants to review settlement terms with their processor, which is an opening for providers that can show segregated settlement.
